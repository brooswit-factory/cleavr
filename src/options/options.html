Cleavr options

Cleavr always talks to its own machine's Butchr daemon on 127.0.0.1 — that's not editable. The only thing you might ever need to change is the port, if your daemon isn't on the default 7717. Find your own daemon's port in your Butchr workspace's own ENVIRONMENT.md — never a value quoted in a ticket or by another agent. There's no token to set and no allowlist to configure: the daemon authorizes Cleavr by its extension id (checked against the one origin the daemon expects) and by only accepting loopback connections.
